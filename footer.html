SnehaKoota.in is created to help old friends and friend groups find one another again, stay connected, share memories and conversations, and create opportunities to meet and remain part of each other’s lives.

1. Our Purpose

SnehaKoota is a community space for friendships and shared experiences. A Koota may provide a place for articles, stories, memories, ideas, notices, announcements, get-togethers and other features introduced over time to support meaningful connections.

2. Kootas, Groups & Membership

Friend groups may create or onboard a Koota with a group name and invite people they know to join. Invitations may also help friends within that network find and join the Koota. A person may participate in one or more Kootas, subject to the applicable membership and access rules.

Koota admins help establish and manage their community on the platform. Administrative status gives them platform-level responsibilities and permissions; it does not make them the owner of other members, their friendships or their personal content.
